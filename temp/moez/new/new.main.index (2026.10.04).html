About

I work on teaching and researching quantum informatics and quantum algorithms, with a research background in programming languages theory and the categorical foundations of object-oriented type systems and quantum processes.

My research combines category theory, formal models, and software engineering to develop foundations, algorithms, and tools for quantum computing and beyond.

I am the author of QAL (Quantum Algorithms Lab), a visual interactive web application for teaching and researching quantum algorithms.

Alongside building QAL, I am working on a broader effort to establish a Quantum Informatics Lab (QI Lab) at IRI, SRTA-City.

QAL — Quantum Algorithms Lab

QAL Algorithm Presentations

QAL offers 10+ (more than ten) algorithm presentations, each presenting an intuitive interactive visualization of quantum algorithms.

H⊗3T⊗3H⊗3   (3 qubits; 3 steps)

To change their algorithms, QAL users can interact directly with algorithm presentations via taps/clicks and drags/drops, or indirectly via the QAL Dashboard.

Algorithm presentations offer instantaneous near-realtime feedback on changes made by the QAL user to the quantum algorithm. (Same also for Final State Histogram.)

QAL Lite, the lightweight edition of QAL, offers three basic algorithm presentations (namely, matrices, standard heatmaps, and circuits. Last two shown in screenshot above.)

QAL is an innovative visual interactive web application for teaching and researching quantum algorithms and related mathematical fields.

  • Interactive quantum circuits and visualizations.

  • Algebraic, structural, and spectral analysis.

  • Algorithm implementations.

  • Educational modules and tutorials.

  • Open-source and continuously evolving. Includes patentable software technology (novel interactive visualizations, in advanced QAL algorithm presentations).

QAL is the platform behind multiple current short/tutorial and regular courses on quantum algorithms and quantum informatics, and is introduced in a 2026 preprint.
